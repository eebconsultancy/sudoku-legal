1. Introduction

EEB CONSULTANCY LLC ("we," "us," or "our") provides Classic Sudoku: Daily Puzzles (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the App.

We aim to provide an enjoyable Sudoku experience while respecting your privacy and giving you appropriate control over advertising and privacy preferences.

2. Information You Provide

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to play Sudoku.

If you voluntarily contact us, we may receive your email address and the information included in your message. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and provide support.

3. Game Progress and Local Storage

The App stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, which may include:

  • Saved puzzles and entered numbers
  • Difficulty levels and unlocked levels
  • Game statistics and completion history
  • Daily challenge progress and streaks
  • Hints, rewards, and gameplay progress
  • Preferences and appearance settings

This information is used to maintain your game progress and provide gameplay features. We do not require an account to store this information.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App may display advertising provided by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google. Advertising formats may include banner ads, interstitial ads, and optional rewarded ads.

Rewarded ads allow you to voluntarily watch an advertisement in exchange for certain in-game benefits, such as an additional hint or another chance to continue a puzzle.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers, where permitted
  • Device information, operating system, and app information
  • Advertising impressions, interactions, and performance data
  • Diagnostic information related to advertising services
  • Consent choices and applicable privacy signals

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize ads.

The types of data processed and their purposes depend on applicable laws, device settings, consent choices, and Google's advertising practices.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services .

5. Consent and Tracking Preferences

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy choices related to advertising and consent.

On supported Apple devices, the App may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before engaging in tracking that requires your authorization.

If you decline tracking permission, the App remains playable. Advertising may still be displayed, but tracking requiring ATT authorization will not be permitted.

Where applicable, you can review or update advertising privacy choices through the Privacy Options feature in the App's Settings.

You may also manage tracking permissions through your device's privacy settings. The availability of particular privacy controls may depend on your region, device, and applicable requirements.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information directly to data brokers.

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described in this Policy and their respective privacy notices.

Information you voluntarily provide through support communications may be processed by the services used to receive and respond to those messages.

We may disclose information when required by law, to comply with legal obligations, or to protect our legal rights.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game progress may remain on your device until you delete it using available App or device controls. Uninstalling the App generally removes its local data, subject to device backup and restoration behavior.

Advertising-related data processed by Google may be retained according to Google's applicable retention practices. We do not directly control all data retained by third-party advertising services.

If you contact us, we may retain your correspondence as reasonably necessary to address your inquiry and comply with applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us using the details below regarding privacy questions or requests relating to information within our control.
