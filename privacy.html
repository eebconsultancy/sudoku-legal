1. Introduction

EEB CONSULTANCY LLC ("we," "us," or "our") provides Classic Sudoku: Daily Puzzles (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the App.

We aim to provide an enjoyable Sudoku experience while respecting your privacy and giving you appropriate control over advertising and privacy preferences.

The App offers free gameplay, optional Premium subscriptions, and a one-time Lifetime Remove Ads purchase.

2. Information You Provide

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to play Sudoku.

You do not need to create a separate account with us to purchase or restore Premium subscriptions or Lifetime Remove Ads.

If you voluntarily contact us, we may receive your email address and the information included in your message. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and provide support.

3. Game Progress and Local Storage

The App stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, which may include:

  • Saved puzzles and entered numbers
  • Difficulty levels and unlocked levels
  • Game statistics and completion history
  • Daily challenge progress and streaks
  • Hints, rewards, and gameplay progress
  • Preferences and appearance settings
  • Onboarding completion status
  • Purchase entitlement status and related preferences

This information is used to maintain your game progress, provide gameplay features, remember your preferences, and enable features associated with valid purchases.

We do not require an account to store this information. Gameplay progress is stored locally and is not transmitted to our own servers.

Restoring purchases does not restore locally stored gameplay progress, statistics, saved puzzles, or streaks.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The App offers optional in-app purchases, including:

  • Premium Weekly subscription
  • Premium Yearly subscription
  • Lifetime Remove Ads one-time purchase

Purchases, subscriptions, free trials, billing, and payment processing are handled by Apple through the App Store and Apple's StoreKit framework.

We do not directly collect, process, or store your payment card number, bank account details, Apple Account password, or other payment credentials.

To provide purchased features, the App may access and process purchase-related information made available through Apple's StoreKit services, including:

  • Purchased product identifiers
  • Transaction identifiers and verification information
  • Subscription and purchase entitlement status
  • Purchase and subscription expiration dates
  • Free trial and introductory offer eligibility
  • Refund or purchase revocation status

This information is used to:

  • Verify purchases and subscriptions
  • Enable purchased gameplay features
  • Disable advertising for entitled users
  • Restore eligible previous purchases
  • Determine applicable subscription benefits
  • Maintain accurate purchase entitlement status

Purchase verification and entitlement management are handled through Apple's StoreKit services and local App logic.

We do not use purchase transaction information to build advertising profiles or provide it to advertising partners for targeted advertising.

Apple processes purchase and payment information according to its own privacy practices.

For more information, see Apple's Privacy Policy .

Additional information about subscription pricing, renewal, cancellation, and purchase conditions is available in our Terms of Use .

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version of the App may display advertising provided by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google. Advertising formats may include banner ads, interstitial ads, and optional rewarded ads.

Rewarded ads allow free users to voluntarily watch an advertisement in exchange for certain in-game benefits, such as an additional hint or another chance to continue a puzzle.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers, where permitted
  • Device information, operating system, and app information
  • Advertising impressions and advertising data
  • Product interactions and advertising performance data
  • Crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic information
  • Consent choices and applicable privacy signals

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, support advertising service functionality, perform analytics, and, where permitted, personalize ads.

The types of data processed and their purposes depend on applicable laws, device settings, consent choices, and Google's advertising practices.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services .

6. Ad-Free Experience for Paid Users

Users with an active Premium subscription, an active eligible Premium free trial, or a valid Lifetime Remove Ads purchase receive an ad-free gameplay experience.

This includes the removal of banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements.

Applicable hints and Second Chance benefits are provided according to the user's purchase entitlement without requiring advertisements.

While a valid ad-free entitlement is recognized, the App does not request new advertisements for that user.

Purchasing an ad-free entitlement does not automatically delete advertising-related information previously processed by Google or its advertising partners.

Previously processed information remains subject to the applicable service provider's privacy and retention practices.

If a Premium subscription expires or an ad-free entitlement is revoked, advertising may resume unless another valid ad-free entitlement remains active.

7. Consent and Tracking Preferences

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy choices related to advertising and consent.

On supported Apple devices, the App may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before engaging in tracking that requires your authorization.

If you decline tracking permission, the App remains playable. Advertising may still be displayed to free users, but tracking requiring ATT authorization will not be permitted.

Where applicable, you can review or update advertising privacy choices through the Privacy Options feature in the App's Settings.

You may also manage tracking permissions through your device's privacy settings.

Declining tracking permission does not prevent you from purchasing or using Premium subscriptions or Lifetime Remove Ads.

The availability of particular privacy controls may depend on your region, device, purchase entitlement, and applicable requirements.
