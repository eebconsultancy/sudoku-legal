1. Introduction

EEB CONSULTANCY LLC ("we," "us," or "our") provides Classic Sudoku: Daily Puzzles (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

We respect your privacy and aim to provide a straightforward puzzle experience.

2. Information You Provide

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to play Sudoku.

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we may receive your email address and the information you include in your message. We use that information to respond to your inquiry.

3. Game Progress and Local Storage

The App may store game-related information on your device, including:

  • Saved puzzles and entered numbers
  • Difficulty levels and unlocked levels
  • Game statistics and completion history

This information supports gameplay, progress tracking, and personalization within the App.

If we introduce advertising, analytics, or additional data-processing services in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information you voluntarily send to us may be processed by the communication services used to receive and respond to your inquiry.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress stored locally may remain on your device until you remove it through available App or device controls. Uninstalling the App generally removes its locally stored data, subject to applicable device backup and restoration behavior.

If you contact us, we may retain your correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to address your inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not require registration or the submission of personal information to play.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support inquiry, please contact us so we can review the request.
